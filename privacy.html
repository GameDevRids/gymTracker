1. Who we are

GymPulse is gym management software provided by [Rids Gym], [address, city, state], India ("we", "us"). You can reach us at [contact email].

2. Whose data, and who decides

GymPulse is used by gym owners and their staff to keep records about their members.

  • Members' data is entered and controlled by the gym. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") the gym is the Data Fiduciary for it, and we process it on the gym's behalf, only to run GymPulse for that gym.
  • Gym owners' and staff accounts (email address, sign-in details) are data we are responsible for ourselves.

If you are a gym member and have a question about your data, please ask your gym first. We will help the gym answer you.

3. What GymPulse stores

About gym owners and staff

  • Email address, an encrypted password (we never see it), and your role in the gym.
  • The date you agreed to these terms.

About the gym

  • Gym name, phone, address, UPI ID and name shown to payers, language and settings.

About members (entered by the gym)

  • Name, mobile number and member code; optionally email, gender, date of birth, emergency contact and notes.
  • Membership plans, dates, fees, payments recorded (amount, method, UPI reference number) and receipts.
  • Check-in times and how they were made (QR card, entrance poster or by staff).
  • Whether the member agreed to WhatsApp messages, and when that changed.
  • A photo, only if the member agreed to it. The agreement is recorded.
  • For entrance-poster check-ins: a random code stored in a cookie on the member's phone, and a short label such as "Android · Chrome". We do not read anything else on the phone.

About people who enquire (entered by the gym)

  • Name, mobile number, the plan they asked about, how they heard about the gym, a note, follow-up dates, and whether they agreed to WhatsApp messages (they answer on the gym's phone themselves) and when.

GymPulse does not take card or bank details. UPI payments go directly from the member's UPI app to the gym.

4. Why we use it

  • To provide GymPulse to the gym: keeping member, membership, payment and attendance records, showing reminders and reports.
  • To sign you in, keep your account secure and stop misuse (for example, by limiting repeated sign-in attempts).
  • To find and fix errors (see section 6).
  • To reply when you contact us, including through "Report a problem".

We do not sell personal data, show advertising, or use members' data for our own marketing.

5. Consent and members' rights

The gym is responsible for telling its members what it records and for having a lawful reason to do so. GymPulse helps by keeping WhatsApp and photo consent off until the member agrees, and by recording each change. If a member is under 18, the gym must have the consent of a parent or guardian.

Members can ask their gym to see, correct or delete their data, or to withdraw consent. Gym owners can correct or remove records in GymPulse, and download all of the gym's data as an Excel file in Settings.

6. Who else handles the data

We use these service providers to run GymPulse. Each only gets what it needs:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and photo storage. Data is stored in [Supabase region, e.g. Mumbai (ap-south-1)].
  • Render — runs the GymPulse server [Render region].
  • Sentry — error reports, if enabled. Before a report is sent, GymPulse removes phone numbers, email addresses, names in quotes, sign-in tokens, IP addresses and the content of forms.
  • Email delivery [SMTP provider] — only to send password-reset emails.

WhatsApp messages are opened in the gym's own WhatsApp app with the text filled in; GymPulse does not send them. "Report a problem" opens a WhatsApp chat with us from your phone; what you send is shared with WhatsApp under its own policy.

Some of these providers may process data outside India. We will only use providers that protect it as this policy describes, and we will follow any restrictions the Government of India sets on transfers.

7. Cookies

GymPulse uses only cookies it needs to work: one that keeps you signed in, one on the demo site, and the member-phone cookie described in section 3. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. The app also stores your screen preferences in your browser.

8. How long we keep data

  • While the gym uses GymPulse, its records are kept until the gym removes them.
  • Enquiry details of people who did not join are deleted automatically 90 days after the enquiry was last changed, and the gym can delete one at any time.
  • When the owner deletes the gym account, the gym is locked at once and everything (members, enquiries, payments, attendance, photos and staff accounts) is erased 7 days later, unless the owner cancels in that time.
  • GymPulse's database provider does not keep automatic backups on our current plan, so data that is deleted or erased cannot be recovered.
  • Server logs hold no member names or phone numbers and are kept for [number] days.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each gym can see only its own data, which the database enforces. Photos are private and shown through links that expire after an hour. If a breach affects personal data, we will inform the affected gyms and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.
